We build tools for testing 3GPP Mission Critical systems: MCPTT, MCData and MCVideo.

**ShallTrace by UIMS** is our clause-by-clause MCX conformance testing: a client emulator that calls through your MCX server, and a passive probe that reads your pcap captures. Each verdict cites the clause, the specification version and the frame, and quotes the requirement. You can start with a free Trace Check at **[uims.eu](https://uims.eu/)**.

### Open source

**[mcx-anonymizer](https://github.com/UIMS-EU/mcx-anonymizer)** · preview 0.1.0 · GPL-3.0-or-later<br>
Anonymises pcap and pcapng captures of MCPTT, MCData and MCVideo traffic on your own machine, so that you can share them with a vendor, a test lab or an AI tool without exposing your network. Fail closed, offline, and every rule cites the 3GPP, ETSI or IETF clause it implements.

Bug reports and pull requests are welcome. Each repository's `CONTRIBUTING.md` explains how to contribute, and its `SECURITY.md` explains how to report a security problem privately.
